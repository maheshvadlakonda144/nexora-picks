Privacy Policy

Learn how NEXORA PICKS handles information provided through our platform.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

NEXORA PICKS operates an online marketplace where customers can discover products and sellers can manage their products and orders. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you use our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our platform, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address
  • Order information
  • Seller and store information

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Process and manage orders
  • Provide customer support
  • Manage seller accounts
  • Improve our website and services
  • Communicate important information about orders or accounts

4. Orders and Sellers

When a customer places an order, relevant order and delivery information may be shared with the seller responsible for fulfilling that order. This information is used to process, manage and deliver customer orders.

5. Payments

NEXORA PICKS may offer different payment methods. Payment information may be processed by third-party payment providers. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any payment provider used during a transaction.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect information stored through our platform. However, no online system can guarantee complete security. Users should also protect their account credentials and passwords.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services to provide features such as authentication, database services, hosting or payment processing. Those third-party services may have their own privacy policies.

8. Your Responsibilities

Users are responsible for providing accurate information when creating accounts, placing orders or registering as sellers. Sellers are responsible for handling customer information only for legitimate order and business purposes.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

NEXORA PICKS may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
