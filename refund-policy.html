Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At NEXORA PICKS, customer satisfaction is important to us. This Refund Policy explains the conditions under which customers may request a refund for products purchased through our marketplace.

1. Refund Eligibility

Customers may be eligible for a refund in situations including:

  • The product received is damaged or defective.
  • The wrong product was delivered.
  • The product is significantly different from the product description.
  • The order was cancelled according to our cancellation policy.
  • The product was not delivered within the applicable delivery period.

2. Refund Request

To request a refund, customers should contact NEXORA PICKS and provide relevant order information, including the order ID and details about the issue.

Customers may also be required to provide photographs or other evidence for damaged, defective, or incorrect products.

3. Return of Products

In some cases, customers may be required to return the product before a refund is processed.

Products should be returned in their original condition where applicable, including original packaging and accessories.

4. Refund Processing

Once a refund request is approved, the refund will be processed using the original payment method whenever possible.

Refund processing times may vary depending on the payment provider, bank, or payment method used.

5. Non-Refundable Items

Some products may not be eligible for refunds, including:

  • Products damaged due to misuse by the customer.
  • Products returned after the applicable return period.
  • Products that are not eligible for return due to hygiene or other applicable reasons.
  • Digital or special products where refunds are not applicable.

6. Seller Responsibility

NEXORA PICKS is a marketplace that connects customers with independent sellers.

Sellers may be responsible for resolving product-related issues, including replacement, return, or refund requests, depending on the circumstances of the order.

7. Changes to This Policy

NEXORA PICKS may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.
